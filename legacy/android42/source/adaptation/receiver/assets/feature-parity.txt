DiPlay Android 4.2 / API17 — wired public source edition

USB-only entry points; no log upload or application-update implementation.
Retains USBMUX/Lockdown/iAP2/NCM with VPN consent; ARMv7 USB/crypto JNI;
H.264 native decryption and bounded video queue; Surface/Texture comparison;
local media self-test and four-trial black-screen diagnostics.

Projection resolution, frame rate and output preferences persist locally and apply
on reconnect. Detects Android-reported display metrics. Defaults: 800x480 / 30fps.
Vehicle-specific audio, microphone, steering keys and GPS still require validation.

Reports are redacted and saved locally. Export includes retained trial reports.
No real phone pairings, runtime accessory identity or signing keys are in source.
A source-only build without externally supplied compatible identity cannot complete
iPhone authentication. Host tests cannot establish real-vehicle compatibility.
